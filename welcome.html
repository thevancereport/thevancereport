One short email each weekday evening, and nothing else. No tips, no price targets, no "buy now".

01 / WHAT ARRIVESThe email.

  • The ten highest-ranked US companies on the Vance Value Screener, with each one's score and sector.
  • What changed: which companies entered or left the top ten since the day before.
  • A link to every company's page, so you can see the four scores and the figures behind them.

It goes out after the screen runs on that day's closing prices, at about 7:15 PM Central. There is no email on weekends or market holidays.

02 / THE RANKINGFour questions, about 1,200 companies.

Every eligible US company is scored on the four questions a value investor asks: is it cheap, is it a good business, is it safe, and has the selling stopped? Each is compared with companies in its own sector, from figures in its own SEC filings. How the Vance Value Screener works explains all of it in about six minutes.

A high rank is a reason to look, not a reason to buy.

We tested the ranking, and we publish what the testing found, including where it falls short. That is on the same page.

03 / ONE THING TO DOKeep it out of spam.

If the confirmation email landed in spam or Promotions, move it to your inbox, or add the sender to your contacts. That is what makes sure the daily emails arrive.

04 / WHILE YOU WAITWhere to start.

  • Today's screen: the current top of the ranking.
  • Look up any company: rank, four scores and every figure behind them.
  • Field notes: longer research, written from the filings.
  • The Vance Report on YouTube: the notes as short videos.

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and it takes one click.
