06Intended Time Horizon

The screening conditions on this Site are built around balance-sheet figures that change only when a company files. Nothing on this Site is intended for day trading, swing trading, or short-term trading of any kind, and no content should be read as a short-term signal. Where a security is discussed, it is discussed on a three to six month view.

A balance sheet is not a catalyst. Clearing this Site's conditions says nothing about what a security does tomorrow, this week, or this month, and a security may stop meeting those conditions at any time without notice.

07High-Risk Securities Warning

This Site's screening methodology specifically surfaces securities that have recently experienced sharp price declines. Securities in this category frequently exhibit elevated volatility, wide bid-ask spreads, thin trading volume, going-concern uncertainty, elevated risk of dilution or delisting, and materially higher risk of total loss of principal than the broader market. Clearing a balance-sheet screen (such as a cash-to-price threshold) does not indicate the security is safe, undervalued, or likely to recover, and does not offset these risks.

You should be prepared to lose your entire investment in any security discussed on this Site.
